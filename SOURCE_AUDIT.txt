Published method
----------------
https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/f4d6932b6b9eec9b9d595e2847d095c1-Paper-Conference.pdf
Verified pp. 3–4: normalized yes/no component scores; PTCV weight 0.45;
RC weight 0.55; strict final decision score > 0.54.

Data correspondence
-------------------
The executed audit uses two IPAD files and four original OUTFOX files. All
immutable URLs, digests, byte sizes and cross-check counts are recorded in
analysis/corpus_audit.json. Source and generation-context hashes appear in
analysis/source_groups.csv. Exact correspondence does not independently prove
human authorship, near-duplicate independence or exclusion from training.
Raw passages are cached locally, not committed.

Statistics
----------
https://itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbino.htm
The zero-error upper bound follows by solving (1-p)^n = 0.05. Calculations are
in analysis/design_calculations.json. Independent software oracles and versions
are in validation/independent_crosscheck.json. The score counterexample is
constructed, not detector output.
